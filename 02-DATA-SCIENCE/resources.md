# Data Science Resources

## Primary repositories

- [Devparihar5/Complete-Data-Science-Roadmap](https://github.com/Devparihar5/Complete-Data-Science-Roadmap) - Python, NumPy, Pandas, visualization, statistics, ML, NLP, CV, Tableau, SQL, PySpark, Azure, projects, and Git.
- [aishwaryanevrekar/The-Complete-DS-Guide](https://github.com/aishwaryanevrekar/The-Complete-DS-Guide) - Python, SQL, statistics, ML, projects, and career preparation.
- [microsoft/Data-Science-For-Beginners](https://github.com/microsoft/Data-Science-For-Beginners) - structured beginner curriculum with projects and quizzes.

## Recommended order

1. `NumPy/` and `Pandas/`: manipulate data confidently.
2. `EDA/`: inspect distributions, quality, missingness, and relationships.
3. `Visualization/`: communicate findings with Matplotlib and Plotly.
4. `SQL/`: query data where it lives.
5. `Tableau/`: build decision-focused dashboards.
6. `projects/`: complete end-to-end analyses with a written conclusion.
